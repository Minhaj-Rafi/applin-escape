# Privacy

Applin Escape 5.3.1 does not require an account, contain advertising or send
telemetry. Gameplay records, settings, saves, player aliases, exported cards and
support reports remain on the local computer unless the player chooses to share
an exported file.

On Windows, normal save data is stored under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ApplinEscape`.
Removing the game folder does not automatically remove those saves. The support
report intentionally omits player aliases, save contents, paths and raw crash
logs. `last_error.txt` stays local and may contain technical platform details;
review it before sharing it publicly.

Challenge codes describe a maze and its rules. They do not contain an account,
player name or saved progress.
